Full-screen alarm experience

Give important tasks the attention they deserve with clear, full-screen reminders, haptics, and immediate actions.

Customizable nag intervals

Choose how persistent Naggy should be — from every minute to every hour — and build a rhythm that works for you.

Natural-language fast add

Type “Take vitamins in 5 minutes” or “Pay the bill tomorrow at 10 AM.” Get a task down fast.

Local-first SQLite engine

Your tasks stay on your phone. No logins, remote task servers, subscription paywalls, or internet connection required.

Subtasks, notes & routines

Break complex items into bite-sized steps with progress tracking, tags, priorities, and custom quiet hours.

In-app GitHub updates

Check for new releases in Settings and download the latest APK with a tap when an update is available.

The Naggy principle

Dismissal is not completion.

Most reminders disappear when you swipe them away. Naggy understands the difference between “I saw it” and “I did it.” Snooze, reschedule, or mark a task done — until then, it stays on your radar.

Good to know

A few quick answers.

Everything you need to know before your first nudge.

Is Naggy free?

Yes. Naggy is free and open source. You can inspect the code and follow development on GitHub.

Does Naggy need an account or internet?

No account is required. Tasks are stored locally on your device and core task management works offline. Internet is used to check and download GitHub releases.

Why does Naggy need notification and alarm permissions?

These permissions help reminders appear at the right time. Exact behavior can vary depending on your Android version and device settings.

Is Naggy available for iPhone?

This page currently provides the Android APK. Check the GitHub repository for the latest platform and release information.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Open an issue in the project's GitHub repository. Bug reports and thoughtful feature requests are welcome.
